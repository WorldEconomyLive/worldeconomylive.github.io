    How this works

    The score

    Each economy gets up to four pillars: activity (factory output growth), jobs (change in unemployment over 12 months, inverted), prices (distance of inflation from 2%, inverted) and trade (port cargo vs a year ago). Each pillar is compared with that country's own last 10 years as a z-score, so +1 means one standard deviation healthier than usual. The score is 50 + 15 × the average pillar, capped at 0–100.

    60+ Strong · 45–60 Steady · 35–45 Soft · under 35 Stressed. It is a summary, not a forecast. Always check the parts behind it.

    Data sources and status

    IMF PortWatch (port and chokepoint data), FRED / U.S. agencies (FX, rates, prices), index owners (S&P, Nasdaq, Nikkei; % changes shown), Eurostat, OECD and BIS via DBnomics, World Bank. Port data lags: the latest 10 days are excluded.
