    10Method

    The score

    Up to four pillars per economy: output (factory output growth, or GDP growth, blended with electricity demand), jobs (12-month change in unemployment, inverted), prices (distance of inflation from 2%, inverted) and trade (port cargo vs a year ago). Each is compared with that country's own last 10 years using the median and median absolute deviation, leaving out the pandemic distortion (March 2020 to December 2021). An economy needs at least two pillars to be scored.

    The world score is 80% the GDP-weighted average of economy scores and 20% global stress: oil and gas price shocks against their own 15-year history, and world port cargo vs a year ago.
